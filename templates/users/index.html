About StockVault

Built with a little
more intention.

StockVault is a university final project focused on making personal portfolio tracking more organized and approachable for Nepal’s stock-market community.

It brings the practical parts of an investment routine into one place: recording holdings, following price ranges, keeping a shortlist, and understanding estimated trade charges.

What can I keep track of in StockVault?+

You can record share holdings and purchase costs, review your portfolio overview, save companies to a wishlist, and set buy or sell price ranges. The trade calculator helps you estimate costs and sale proceeds.

What do I need to add a holding?+

Choose a listed company and enter the number of shares, buying rate per share, and purchase type: IPO or secondary market. Your portfolio brings the holding’s details together with available market-price data.

How is a wishlist different from a price target?+

A wishlist saves companies you may want to revisit. A price target adds a low and high price range with a buy or sell intent, so you can compare your plan with the available market snapshot.

How does the trade calculator work?+

Enter your share quantity and purchase price. For a sale estimate, add a selling price and the applicable holding period or investor type. The breakdown uses StockVault’s configured brokerage, SEBON fee, DP charge, and capital gains tax settings.

When are market prices updated?+

Portfolio and target pages use the market data available when you load the page. Refresh the page to request another snapshot. Availability depends on the underlying market-data source.

Which currency is used?+

Portfolio figures, price targets, and trade estimates are presented in Nepalese rupees (NPR).

Can I place trades through StockVault?+

Use StockVault to organize your holdings and plan with price ranges and cost estimates. Actual stock purchases and sales are placed through your broker.
